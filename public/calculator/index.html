A little room to solve.

Everything runs offline. Your workspace saves in this browser; use Save / open to keep a portable copy.

  • Expressions: y=x^2, sin(x), x=3, or sqrt(81)+5!.
  • Points: (1,2), A=(1,2), A(1,2).
  • Functions: f(x)=x^2+1, then f(3). Use a=2 for a slider.
  • Implicit curves: x^2+y^2=25. Inequalities: y<x^2 or x+y>=2. Shading is approximate; strict bounds are dashed.
  • Roots and modulus: sqrt(81)=9, nthrt(27,3)=3, nthrt(-8,3)=-2. Absolute-value bars work: |x-2| and ||-3||. mod(7,3)=1 gives the remainder.
  • Distance: distance((1,2),(4,6))=5, or define A(1,2) and B(4,6), then distance(A,B). Named point labels are case-sensitive inside distance.
  • Tables: type table in an expression to show the table. Delete that expression to hide the table and its plotted points; your values stay saved. Then enter x1 / y1 pairs, generate values with f(x), or import two CSV columns. Generated values are read-only until you clear the formula.
  • Fits: y1 ~ m*x1+b or y1 ~ p*x1^2+q*x1+r. Unknown parameters must enter the model linearly. a1 aliases y1; a1 ~ x compares to the fixed line y=x.
  • Analyze: find approximate roots, turning points, and the y-intercept of a visible function within the current x range.
  • More math: cbrt, sign, sinh, cosh, tanh, hypot, mod, ncr, npr, and log(value,base). Example: ncr(10,3) = 120.
  • Navigate: drag / pinch to move and zoom. On the graph, arrow keys pan, +/− zoom, Home resets. Click points or explicit curves to trace and estimate nearby intersections.

Numerical plotting and analysis are approximate. Very narrow features and rapid oscillations can be missed. Domain restrictions, piecewise notation, symbolic algebra, and nonlinear parameter fitting are not supported. This independent calculator is not affiliated with Desmos or College Board.
